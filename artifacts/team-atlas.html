The Campaign Teardown.

Per masterdoc §11.5: every Campaign renders as a four-tab Species Experience. The same kernel that makes OKG's Species Experience legible — Photo / Blueprint / Intelligence / Compare — applied to marketing as Creative / Anatomy / Intelligence / Compare.

Tab 01

Creative.

The raw asset. Image, video, copy, landing page screenshot, podcast embed. No annotations. If we don't have rights, we show a placeholder; the structured anatomy still works.

Tab 02

Anatomy.

Engineering-style annotations on the creative: dimension lines on copy length, callouts on the CTA, brackets on the audience cue, copper underlines on framework references.

Tab 03

Intelligence.

Three rows: benchmarks the campaign was measured against, actual measured performance, frameworks cited (Ehrenberg-Bass, MSI, IPA effectiveness). Every row carries a source URL.

Tab 04

Compare.

The signature device. Draggable slider showing two views of the same artifact: Creative on the left, Anatomy overlay on the right. OKG's photo↔blueprint pattern, applied to marketing.

Anti-fabrication — codified.

Marketing is the domain most prone to LLM hallucination. Schema enforces the discipline.

No invented numbers.

Schema constraint: benchmarks.source_id is NOT NULL; metrics.source_kind is NOT NULL. No platform-reported / inferred / claimed-by-brand label, no row.

No invented attribution.

Show the framework and the assumption explicitly. If we don't know if MMM, last-click, or platform-reported is the basis, the metric carries a confidence flag.

No publishing without rights.

We own it, the brand released it publicly, or we show only the structured anatomy without the creative. Cycle 002 BKG teardown is owned content.

No success without success metric.

"Successful" alone is meaningless. "Hit a 4.2× ROAS over 30 days against a 3.0× WordStream search benchmark" is meaningful.

Sacred rules.

Inherited from the umbrella masterdoc v2 §7 and 09_LESSONS.md. Violate any and the work is rejected. MKG inherits the visual DNA — voice over-corrects toward restraint because marketing is the domain most prone to bombast.

L-001 · Sacred

Parchment, never dark.

Background #f5f0e8. Cream #FBF8F3 for cards. Garden Wars is the one exception — it's intentionally a dark strategy tool.

L-026 · Promoted from TKG

Bold sans-serif default.

Inter 700–800 for headlines, titles, CTAs. Cormorant Garamond italic via .emphasis-italic, opt-in only. Space Mono uppercase wide-tracked for tech labels.

L-027 · Brand

Motion is the brand.

The orrery rotates. The conveyor belt animates. Reduced-motion media queries are intentionally absent. Attention spans are short; motion holds them.

L-005 · Signature

Victorian engineering signature.

Every public surface includes at least one of: dimension-line annotation, hand-drawn flourish, gear/compass ornament, source-citation footer, graph visualization texture.

Voice rules.

Do say.

  • Evidence suggests…
  • Verified against three sources.
  • Updated 4 hours ago.
  • Ground truth for [domain].
  • The knowledge graph connects…

Don't say.

  • We think… / Probably… / Our AI believes…
  • Trust us.
  • Cutting-edge / innovative / leveraging synergies.
  • Disrupting [industry].
  • Amazing / exciting / revolutionary.

The five metrics that report up.

Citation health delta.

Re-run four canonical questions + four long-tail. Count MKG URLs in top 10 web; count LLM citations. North-star.

Cross-link density.

Outbound links per Campaign (floor ≥2). Cross-garden links tracked separately. Federation moat layer 2.5.4.

Source-triangle completeness.

% of Campaigns with all three of Authority + Observation + Commerce sources. Below 90% = Critic escalation.

RSI heartbeat freshness.

Count of Campaigns updated in last 30d via programmatic ingestion. Without growth here, the garden is a brochure.

MCP retrieval count.

Distinct external agents that retrieved an MKG entity in the last cycle. Slope > level.

Compounding score.

Single number reported every cycle. Cycle 001 baseline = 254.6. Cycle 002 target = beat it.
