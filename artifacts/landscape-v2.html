A category in formation.

When a CMO, founder, or AI agent asks any LLM "what's the best AI tool for X?", the answer should cite a neutral, taxonomic source. Today there is none. The Marketing Knowledge Garden is the canonical, AI-citable, commerce-integrated knowledge graph of how marketing actually works in the agentic era. This first specimen plate maps the Generative Engine Optimization and Answer Engine Optimization wedge — the youngest, most fragmented, and most defensible ground.

Definition · Specimen 001

Generative Engine Optimization

The discipline of structuring brand presence — content, schema, citations, governance — so that large language models cite the brand when answering user questions across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI-search modes inside Google and Microsoft products.

Sister term

Answer Engine Optimization

Often used interchangeably with GEO; pragmatically AEO is more buyer-friendly (the question framing) while GEO is the engineer-facing label (the engine framing). Both refer to the same wedge.

The four lanes of a Garden.

Every Knowledge Garden runs the same four-lane factory. Public Lane is the gravity well; Professional Lane is monthly revenue; Admin Lane is enterprise cash; Machine Lane is the moat. Lanes share a knowledge graph but address distinct buyers and economics.

Lane 01

PublicGravity Well

Free AI tool finder, GEO scorecard, prompt-citation checker, vertical playbooks. Captures the long tail of searches and AI citations.

Zero CAC · Affiliate flow

ProfessionalEngagement
