Definition · Specimen 001

Generative Engine Optimization.

The discipline of structuring brand presence — content, schema, citations, governance — so that large language models cite the brand when answering user questions across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI-search modes inside Google and Microsoft products.

Sister term

Answer Engine Optimization.

Often used interchangeably with GEO. Pragmatically, AEO is buyer-friendly (the question framing); GEO is engineer-facing (the engine framing). Same wedge.

The four lanes of a Garden.

Every Knowledge Garden runs the same four-lane factory. Public Lane is the gravity well; Professional Lane is monthly revenue; Admin Lane is enterprise cash; Machine Lane is the moat. Lanes share a knowledge graph but address distinct buyers and economics.

A single visitor to the public lane can compound through every other lane: a free GEO scorecard turns into a subscription dashboard, into an enterprise vendor-intelligence contract, into an MCP-server data feed consumed by their AI agents. Same knowledge graph, four economics.

The same skeleton ships across every garden — Builder's, Orchid, Health, Toxicology, Marketing. Different verbs, same shape.
