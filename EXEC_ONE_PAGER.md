## What it is
The newest vertical of The Knowledge Gardens. Same architecture as OKG /
BKG / HKG / TKG (four lanes × three surfaces × one knowledge graph). The
canonical, AI-citable knowledge graph of how marketing actually works in
the agentic era.

**Tagline (umbrella).** *The ground truth AI needs to survive.*
**Tagline (MKG).** *The ground truth marketing AI cites.*

## What it sells (eventually)
- **Public Lane.** Free Campaign Teardown — paste a campaign URL, MKG returns a JSON-LD entry in the graph. Zero CAC, citation moat.
- **Professional Lane.** $79 Practitioner / $249 Studio / $799 Studio+ per month. MCP server access, JSON-LD exports, write-back to graph.
- **Admin Lane.** $60K–$240K ACV. Vendor intelligence, M&A target maps, custom landscape reports. Buyer ranking: agency holdcos > PE diligence > F500 CMOs.
- **Machine Lane.** Free community API → $2K/mo Startup → $250K–$2M/yr Enterprise Data License.

## Primary entity
**Campaign.** Not company-entities, not channels, not personas. Channels and assets are facets of campaigns; personas are audiences-of-campaigns. (Masterdoc v2 §11.3.)

## Killer app
**The Campaign Teardown.** Four tabs per campaign — Creative / Anatomy / Intelligence / Compare. Compare slider morphs between raw asset and annotated anatomy. Mirrors OKG's Photo / Blueprint / Intelligence / Compare kernel.

## Top 3 risks
1. **A wedge competitor open-sources a comparable graph first.** Profound ($155M), Bluefish ($68M), or HubSpot AEO have the resources. Mitigation: ship density before they ship neutrality.
2. **Internal campaigns lack variety for taxonomy.** If every record is "founder writes a launch post," our taxonomy is thin. Mitigation: Week 8 audit; pivot to one tier-gated external category if needed.
3. **Anti-fabrication breaks under deadline pressure.** A single fabricated record undermines the platform's authority across every garden. Mitigation: schema-level constraints (codified in v0.2); 90% source-triangle completeness floor; Critic auto-escalation.
